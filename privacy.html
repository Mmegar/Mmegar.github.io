Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

DailyChronicle ("the app") is a personal automation tool run by its owner to upload videos to, and read statistics for, the owner's own YouTube channel.

What data the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app requests permission to:

  • Upload and manage videos on your YouTube channel (YouTube Data API: upload / manage scopes).
  • Read view and performance statistics for your channel (YouTube Analytics API, read-only).

The app only accesses the YouTube channel that you sign in with. It does not access Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

How the data is used and stored

  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, inside the local automation software (n8n). They are never sent to any server run by the app.
  • Channel statistics are used only to decide what to upload next and are kept locally.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

How to revoke access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all uploads and analytics reads immediately. To have locally stored tokens or statistics deleted, email the contact below.
